# Release quality checks

Version 1.0.0 · 3 November 2026

- Validated seven screen templates in three branding modes at 1440 px and 390 px: 42 screen/mode/viewport combinations.
- Checked local image loading, browser script errors, and horizontal page overflow. Wide navigation and tables use their own scroll regions on small screens.
- Checked customer page title, logo, favicon, owner and sender labels, and absence of visible provider identity in customer product screens. The kit review controls and index remain provider materials.
- Checked powered attribution visibility, mode persistence through navigation, demo sign-in, and approval preview behavior.
- Checked primary button contrast in all configured modes.
- Rendered and visually inspected all six pages across the four Word templates and matching PDF exports.
- Checked customer Word/PDF visible text and document metadata for provider references.
- Checked SVG parsing, PNG transparency and sizes, local HTML asset paths, and ZIP integrity.

These checks apply to the supplied templates. Email clients have not been tested; populate and test the email source in the selected delivery platform. Product screens are presentation templates and do not include live operations or authentication. Preferred font binaries are not included; system fallbacks are used.
