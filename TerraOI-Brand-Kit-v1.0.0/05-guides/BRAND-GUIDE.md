# TerraOI brand guide

## Identity

**TerraOI** is the brand name. **Operational Intelligence** is the descriptor. The core tagline is **AI operations for air, surface, and subsurface.** Preserve capitalization, especially the final capital O and I.

The brand is precise, calm, capable, and practical. Describe what people can observe, review, decide, and do. Use grounded language and explain operational value without overstating autonomy.

## Logo system

The symbol has three engineered layers for air, surface, and subsurface. Connected nodes communicate coordinated intelligence. The brass middle node signals the planning and orchestration layer. The outlined geometric wordmark uses original vector paths and requires no installed font.

Use `terraoi-logo-color.svg` on white or mist; use the white master on graphite. Use the black master for single ink reproduction. Full lockups add the descriptor and tagline. Use symbol-only artwork for compact spaces and the simplified favicon artwork at browser sizes. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual components, add glow, or replace the wordmark with typed text.

Maintain clear space around the symbol equal to at least one node diameter, and around the horizontal logo equal to 10% of its height. Suggested minimum widths: horizontal logo 160 px; full lockup 380 px; symbol 40 px. Below 40 px use the simplified favicon. Validate real reproduction sizes before print.

The vector masters are a faithful geometric implementation of the approved visual direction; they refine the raster concept rather than tracing every generated detail. The concept board is a reference, not a source for cropping production logos.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White | #FFFFFF | Main surfaces |
| Mist | #F4F6F7 | Canvas and secondary surfaces |
| Graphite | #17252B | Text and deep backgrounds |
| Slate | #52636B | Supporting text |
| Mineral teal | #007F78 | Primary actions and identity |
| Brass | #A48748 | Orchestration node, lines, and restrained accents |
| Border | #DCE3E6 | Dividers and outlines |

Keep white dominant. Use teal for interaction and brass for small highlights. Brass is not a small-text color on white and is not an operational warning color. Status colors have separate tokens and readable text labels. Use graphite text on pale surfaces and white text on the primary teal. Customer colors must pass contrast review before use.

## Typography and layout

Use Inter for interfaces and headings, and IBM Plex Mono for coordinates, measurements, and IDs. This offline package uses Arial and Consolas fallbacks when preferred fonts are unavailable. Font binaries are not bundled. Install or self-host approved font releases with their license files before a production rollout; font sources are listed in FONT-NOTES.md.

Base interface text is 15–16 px with comfortable line spacing. Headings use clear weight and generous spacing. Base spacing is 4 px, with 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, and 64 px increments. Cards use 12 px corners; controls use 6 px corners. Prefer fine borders and clean hierarchy to heavy shadows.

## Agentic visual language

Use sparse connected nodes, directional paths, and the sequence Observe → Plan → Act. These elements indicate coordination and a path from evidence to action. Keep source evidence, proposed actions, approvals, permissions, and outcomes distinguishable. Do not add robot faces, decorative brains, sparkles, or invented confidence percentages.

Use terrain contours and layered sections to connect air, surface, and subsurface. Avoid busy networks behind text. Directional graphics must stay legible in monochrome.

## Voice

Prefer “Review the proposed inspection plan” to “Let autonomous intelligence handle everything.” Prefer “Approval required” to an unexplained colored dot. Reports must identify evidence, reporting period, scope, limitations, and action status. Marketing may use the core tagline; product messages should describe the user's next step.
