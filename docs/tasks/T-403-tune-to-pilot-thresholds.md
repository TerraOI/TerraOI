## Goal

Every §14.1 threshold is a number somebody has to move, and only three levers are in the build's hands: **prompt versions, chunk shape, chain configuration** (the fourth — a better corpus — is [T-315](./T-315-staging-corpora-eval-cases.md)'s and the leads'). This packet runs [T-312](./T-312-eval-harness.md)'s harness against one pinned corpus generation, changes **one lever at a time**, and reports each before/after pair until both experts clear §14.1's entry table — or until it reports, with numbers, that a threshold will not clear on the pinned chain and the gate decision belongs to the owner (§15.0's cut rule). Its deliverable is a filled §14.1 table in the week-4 register, per expert **and per tier**, because §14.1 says a cheap tier with poor quality must be visible rather than averaged away.

**The thresholds it must clear, from §14.1** (pilot entry, end of week 4):

| Approved eval cases per expert | ≥ 40, of which ≥ 10 must-refuse/clarify and ≥ 5 cross-tenant or project bait | `eval_cases` rows carrying `approvedBy`/`approvedAt`, exported into `evals/<expert>/` by T-312. Today: **114 drafts, zero approved** — approval is a named lead's action (§20.8), not this packet's |
| Isolation / bait cases | 100% pass | the result rows for those case types, over [T-402](./T-402-red-team-pass.md)'s bait material |

## Contract

Consumes, does not change: `ExpertDefinition` (`promptTemplateRef` id + version, per-task `modelChains` validated against that task's `DEFAULT_TASK_CHAIN_ORDER`, `limits`, `minVerifierTier`, pinned `embeddingIndexKey` — [`src/contracts/expert-definition.ts`](../../src/contracts/expert-definition.ts)), `ProviderRoute` and the clearance matrix ([`src/contracts/provider-route.ts`](../../src/contracts/provider-route.ts)), `Answer`/`Claim`/`EvidenceRef`, and the rules written in [docs/contracts/chat-protocol.md](../contracts/chat-protocol.md) and [docs/contracts/model-chain.md](../contracts/model-chain.md).

Five hard rules, each one an acceptance test below rather than an intention:

1. **A measurement is comparable only inside one corpus generation, one approved-set version and one chain configuration.** F35 binds results to the corpus generation, so a before/after pair that crosses generations is void.
2. **A chunk-shape change bumps `parser_generation` and re-embeds into a new index generation** — never an in-place rewrite of `body_text` or of vectors. The immutability of `body_text`, `payload` and `parser_generation` that [T-315](./T-315-staging-corpora-eval-cases.md) records in its Contract section, plus `unique (collection_id, content_hash)`, mean a chunking experiment is a re-run of the corpus, not an update. In week 4 that re-run is **staging-only**, and the generation it produces is named in the register row.
3. **Never relax data policy to make a case passable** (§12.4's last line; AGENTS §4.3, §4.4): no `narrowClearance` widening, no moving a case to a cheaper class, no provider uncleared for the payload's highest class. `restricted` content is not ingested in M1 at all.
4. **Every reported number carries the tier that answered** — F38 returns it, F40 records the eligible chain and every skipped provider with its reason, and [T-313](./T-313-budgets-run-audit.md)'s run audit persists it. This packet may raise `minVerifierTier`; it may not lower it below what the expert's `riskClass` allows (T-311 pins that rule on insert).
5. **Infrastructure failures are excluded from quality numbers and reported separately** — a mid-run provider outage is one of T-312's infrastructure failure classes, and tuning against it optimises for an outage.
